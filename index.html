OP: INDOOR MILSPEED

            
IDMS

            
Indoor Milspeed is the ultimate CQB airsoft simulation, combining milsim realism with speedsoft pace.

            

              
5V5TEAMS

              
3:00PER ROUND

              
MAYHEMGUARANTEED

            

            
Follow @ProjectN1 for the latest IDMS info and scheduling.

          

          

            
A Attack
Complete the objective: threat neutralization or item recovery. Attackers can enter and exit the house as needed and may use grenades to help clear rooms.

            
D Defend
You're surrounded, and your only job is to stay alive. Defenders can't leave the house until the final minute but can barricade to slow the entry.

            
+ Medic
Every player can be revived once. Downed players die in place and can call for a medic. Once a teammate makes contact, both can move to safety to finish the revive.

            
# Ammo Limit
Semi only. One magazine for rifles and SMGs, three shells for shotguns. No mag adapters. One grenade per player per round.

            
G Gas / Spring Only
Gas rifles, SMGs and shotguns; spring shotguns and bolt guns. No pistols. Stocks must be deployed. 350 FPS / 1.14 J limit.

            
⌂ Multi-Entry Shoot House
Attack and defend a house of rooms, hallways, windows and doorways. Attackers can hit from one entry point or several to confuse and overwhelm the defenders.

          

        

      


      
      

        

          

            
OP: CENTER FLAG

            
Jungle Ball

            
Team-vs-team competitive play. Win a round by carrying the center flag into the enemy start zone or by eliminating the entire opposing team.

            

              
5V5TEAMS

              
3:00MAX ROUND

              
BO3PER MATCH

            

          

          

            
T Teams
Five players per team, and multiple team entries are allowed. One substitution per set. Players can't swap between teams. Rosters are locked before games start.

            
⚑ Center Flag
The flag must be carried in hand, not stuffed in a dump pouch. If the carrier is hit, the flag drops right where they were hit.

            
S Semi Only
Any gun type, semi-locked. No burst or ramping. Trigger feathering is allowed; one pull = one shot. Midcaps only. No winders, no M4 mag adapters for pistols.

            
! Penalties
Any penalty forfeits the current round for the team. A fourth team penalty means disqualification for the rest of the day.

          

        

        

          OPEN FULL RULEBOOK // MATCH, HITS, PENALTIES, TOURNAMENT
          

            

              
TIME & MATCH

              
    
                
  • The referee verbally checks both teams are ready; the round starts on the referee's signal.
    • 
                
  • One match is best of 3 rounds, each round 3:00 max. Points are only awarded for winning a round.
    • 
                
  • If neither team is eliminated by 3:00, the round is a draw and no points are awarded. First team to 2 points wins.
    • 
                
  • If Round 1 is won and Round 2 is a draw, Round 3 is played as normal. If Round 3 is another draw, Round 4 is sudden death.
    • 
                
  • If Rounds 1 and 2 are both draws, Round 3 is sudden death: the first team to control the flag or score a kill wins.
    • 
              

              
BREAKOUT

              
    
                
  • Teams break out from their start zones at opposite ends of the field.
    • 
                
  • One foot must be completely inside the start zone.
    • 
                
  • All guns must point at the wall, away from the enemy team, before the round begins.
    • 
              

              
ENGAGEMENT

              
    
                
  • No minimum engagement distance. No surrenders or bang-bang. No melee weapons. No grenades. 350 FPS or 1.14 J limit.
    • 
                
  • Any bodily contact with a BB is a hit. Gun hits and ricochets don't count. Dry fires with an empty barrel don't count.
    • 
                
  • If opposing players shoot each other at the same time, both are out, with no continued play.
    • 
                
  • Referees control the countdown and start/end, can call hits and pull players, and have the final call, period.
    • 
              

            

            

              
PENALTIES

              
    
                
  • All penalties count as team penalties and can't be transferred to another player.
    • 
                
  • Penalties include not calling a hit, late calls or continuing to play after being hit, purposely moving a barrier, and any attempt to gain an unfair advantage (for example, dead players calling out enemy positions).
    • 
                
  • Maximum of 4 penalties per team; the 4th means disqualification for the day. Penalties don't reset between sets or brackets, only at the end of each event.
    • 
              

              
TOURNAMENT

              
    
                
  • Teams need 5+ players, with no maximum roster size. A player can register for only one team.
    • 
                
  • All teams must be registered and paid before the first match set is generated. No walk-ons or new teams after that.
    • 
                
  • Matches are randomly generated on the day in front of all team captains. There are no repeat matchups until every team has played every other team, and every team plays the same number of matches.
    • 
                
  • Double elimination with a blind-pick bracket. Semifinal and final are best of 5; the final winner takes the tournament.
    • 
                
  • A penalty is an automatic round loss; if both teams are penalized at once, the round is a draw.
    • 
                
  • One video challenge per team per tournament. It can only contest the penalty that was called and must be issued right after the round. The team must provide 100% clear proof, and a failed challenge earns a penalty.
    • 
                
  • Rules may be changed by agreement of all parties before the tournament. The rules posted at the start of the first game of the first bracket govern the whole tournament.
    •

[06] // RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

      
Rules & Guidelines

      
We're proud of our field and strictly enforce these rules so everyone plays safe and has fun. Your first and only warning comes at orientation or check-in. Breaking the rules leads to disciplinary action as management sees fit.

    


    

      
■ PRIORITY TRAFFIC

      
Read and understand the rules. They're there to protect everyone and keep things running smoothly. You won't have a good time if you break them. We are not joking.

    


    

      

        
01 Face Protection

        
    
          
  • There's no minimum engagement distance, so full face protection is required for all ages.
    • 
          
  • Players 17 and under must wear a one-piece full face mask. No goggle-and-mesh combos.
    • 
          
  • Players 18+ may use two-piece systems.
    • 
          
  • Helo-kits, shemaghs and neoprene masks don't count and aren't allowed.
    • 
          
  • Ear and head protection is not required, but highly recommended.
    • 
        

      

      

        
02 Engagement

        
    
          
  • 350 FPS or 1.14 joules max.
    • 
          
  • Semi fire only. No ramping, binary, auto or burst (shotguns excepted).
    • 
          
  • No minimum engagement distance (M.E.D.).
    • 
          
  • No bang-bang, surrender or parlay.
    • 
          
  • It only takes one BB hit to be out.
    • 
          
  • Gun hits and ricochets do not count.
    • 
        

      

      

        
03 Fire Discipline

        
    
          
  • No excessive shooting.
    • 
          
  • No jump shooting.
    • 
          
  • No blind firing.
    • 
          
  • No shooting through cracks.
    • 
          
  • No shooting at dead players.
    • 
          
  • No shooting while dead.
    • 
        

      

      

        
04 Field Conduct

        
    
          
  • No talking or signaling to active players while dead.
    • 
          
  • Don't remove your face protection while dead.
    • 
          
  • Do not climb or move barriers.
    • 
          
  • Don't grab another player's weapon or gear.
    • 
          
  • Don't yell at another player that they're hit. Report cheating or foul play to the referee.
    • 
          
  • Turn in anything you find on the field to the referee.
    • 
          
  • No verbal or physical confrontations.
    • 
        

      

      

        
05 Restricted

        
    
          
  • No lasers, grenades or riot shields without prior approval.
    • 
          
  • No melee weapons.
    • 
          
  • No personal recording of gameplay without prior approval.
    • 
          
  • Rules and guidelines are subject to change.
    • 
        

      

      

        
06 Check-In & Waiver

        
    
          
  • Players 18+ need a valid ID at check-in.
    • 
          
  • Players under 18 must be checked in by a parent or legal guardian with valid ID.
    • 
          
  • Minors need a copy of the parent's or guardian's valid CA license. The name and address must match the waiver.
    •

[08] // ORIGIN FILE — DECLASSIFIED

      
Once Upon a Time…

      
A young boy got his first airsoft gun, and with a passion for shooting, he loved it right away. Shooting cans in the garden soon lost its charm, so he went looking for bigger thrills at the nearest airsoft field. The rush of simulated combat and the camaraderie captured his heart. He played nearly every day and made friends for life. Then their field relocated, and things changed.

      
The new field was exciting at first, but as it changed, the joy faded. Visits got less frequent as the game they loved seemed to give way to greed and neglect. When the field's overseers left, the friends had nowhere to play. Unwilling to let their passion die, the boy and his friends came together to build their own arena: Project N1.

      
Now here we stand, welcoming you to join us. Just be ready for a bit of mischief. They're quite fond of it.
